MXLogger Analyzer is a local log analysis tool for developers and testers, designed specifically for .mx log files generated by MXLogger. Import, decrypt, search and analyze logs directly in Chrome to locate errors and trace the events surrounding an issue.

• Import multiple files: Select or drag and drop multiple .mx files, merge their logs and remove duplicate records to investigate issues in one place.

• Decrypt locally: Open both unencrypted and encrypted logs. Configure multiple KEY / IV sets and try them in sequence to handle logs created with different encryption keys.

• Search and filter: Find relevant entries with keyword search and filters for log level, Tag, Name and time range.

• Explore structured JSON: Read complex data in a collapsible JSON tree with syntax highlighting.

• Trace the context: Open the entries before and after a specific log without clearing your current filters, helping you understand how an issue unfolded.

• Inspect and export: Search within individual log details, highlight matches, copy content and export logs as TXT files for investigation and collaboration.

• Personalize your workspace: Choose between English and Chinese, light and dark themes, and customize your font settings.

Log parsing, decryption and queries take place locally. Log content and decryption parameters are not uploaded to a server. Parsed logs are cached in your browser so you can continue viewing them after refreshing. By default, KEY / IV values remain only in the current tab session; you can also choose to save them locally in your browser.

To get started, prepare .mx log files generated by MXLogger. Encrypted logs require the corresponding KEY / IV. After installing the extension, click its icon, select your files and follow the import steps. Core log analysis features work offline.

All features are free to use. The optional “Support the author” feature lets you make a voluntary contribution and does not affect access to any functionality.
